KAI moves

  • Stick Move, jump with up, crouch with down, hold back to block. Double-tap left or right to dash; keep holding forward and the dash becomes a run.
  • P / K Punch and kick. Tap again as it lands to step up to the heavy.
  • SP Special move. Neutral, holding back, or holding down each give a different one. Holding forward gives the heavy version.
  • EX The same, powered up, for 1 bar.
  • SUPER Super at 2 bars, Ultimate at 3. Hold down to pick the super when you have 3.
  • GRAB Throw. Press it as you are grabbed to break free.

Six buttons with the arcade motions shown in the move list. THR is throw; EX P and EX K press two punches or two kicks.

Arrows assume you face right. Every special has an EX version: press two punches (or two kicks) for 1 bar. Dash with a double tap left or right; keep holding forward to run. Overhead for everyone: → + MP. Throw: LP + LK.

System

BLOCKHold back
Lows need down-back. Jump attacks and the overhead must be blocked standing. Specials chip.
PARRYTap toward the hit
Tap → within 8 frames before a strike lands, or ↓ against lows. No damage, and you recover first. A missed tap locks you out for 20 frames.
COUNTERHit their startup
+20% damage and 2 extra frames of hitstun.
PUNISHHit their recovery
+20% damage and 4 extra frames, enough to link moves that normally don't combo.
CANCELNormal → special → super
Punches, st.LK and cr.MK cancel into specials on hit or block. Any special, EX or not, cancels into a super or the Ultimate, on the ground or in the air. Projectile specials cancel in the moment after the shot leaves, hit or not.
LAUNCHcr.HP starts an air combo
Crouching heavy punch launches, and when it hits you leap up after them by yourself (hold back to stay on the ground, or cancel into a special instead). In the air, light and medium hits knock them away from you and chain light → medium → heavy; a heavy punch or kick smashes them straight down. The first smash of a combo bounces them off the floor so you can keep hitting them; a second one slams them down for good.
JUGGLEEight points
A launched body can be hit by anything. Normals and EX cost 1 point, specials 2, supers 3. At 8 it falls free. Some air moves bounce it off the floor.
RUSHTap →→ on contact
Spend 1 bar to cancel a cancellable normal into a fast dash. The next hit gets 4 extra frames, so heavy → rush → heavy combos.
STUNThe bar under your health
Taking hits fills it; it drains when you are left alone. Fill it and you are knocked down and get up dizzy. Mash buttons and wiggle the stick to recover sooner.
LINKPlus frames
st.MP is +6 on hit and cr.MP starts in 6, so it combos with no cancel. Inputs buffer for 5 frames.
THROWBeats blocking
5-frame startup. Press LP+LK within 8 frames of being grabbed to tech it. Hold back to throw behind you.
GAUGEThree bars
EX and rush cancels cost 1, supers 2, the Ultimate all 3. Hitting, blocking and parrying build it, and it carries between rounds.
ULTIMATE↓↙←↓↙← + P or K
A zoom, a flash, then a fast invincible strike. If it lands, even mid-juggle, the full sequence plays. Blocked, you are wide open. Jason's is a running grab instead. Every super and ultimate can be guarded, grabs included.
SCOREArcade mode
Damage scores as you deal it. Winning a round adds time and vitality bonuses, 30,000 for a perfect, and extra for finishing with a super or Ultimate. Continuing resets your score.
